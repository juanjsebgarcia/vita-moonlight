# Turkish (tr) glossary

**Address:** polite "siz" forms (imperatives in -in/-ın: "basın", "seçin"; "PC'niz", "önceliğiniz"), as in PlayStation's Turkish system software. Menu actions (row labels, buttons) use the short verb stem ("Bağlan", "Sil", "...etkinleştir").

**Suffixes on placeholders:** never attach a case suffix directly to `{0}` etc., since the right vowel depends on the name. Use a carrier word instead: "{0} bilgisayarına", "{0} bilgisayarının", "{0} adlı", "{0} için", "{0} üzerinde".

**Percent:** Turkish order, `%{0}`.
